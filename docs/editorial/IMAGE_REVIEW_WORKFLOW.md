## Image options by medium

Present three distinct, researched image candidates, labeled A, B and C, for the original seven media. Photography has the exception below. Choose and verify images as part of the deliverable. Do not ask Julio to find higher-quality sources or alternative images himself.

- **Architecture:** Offer three legible views of the overall building in the selected engraving family. Use whole-building elevations, exterior views or complete cutaways; avoid plan-only and detail-only choices. Use Pantheon A/B as the strongest references. Preserve geometry and identify reconstructions or proposals. Different authentic drawings may use different paper tones.
- **Sculpture:** Offer three verified source views of the correct object. Favor close portraits, visually eye-level views, dark backgrounds and headroom. Source-based edits must be disclosed and include the untouched source. Preserve pose, material, damage and identity; disclose unavoidable viewpoint limits.
- **Theater:** Offer three documented production images, posters, playbills or original edition materials. Identify production and date where verified. Do not substitute film adaptations or invent stage imagery. Theater has no approved fixed thumbnail treatment yet.
- **Painting:** Compare genuine reproductions of the same artwork. Assess resolution, focus, completeness and color differences between sources. Avoid workshop copies, related works and misleading crops. Three resized exports of one file do not count as three options. Do not invent color grades to create artificial choices.
- **Music:** Offer three relevant cover or release-label images. Identify recording, performer and release differences. Use the same approved photographic vinyl surround for each option; replace only the central artwork. A composition and a particular recorded performance are distinct records.
- **Cinema:** Offer three different frames from the correct film/version. Select visually strong scenes related to the work’s central concerns. Use Julio’s authorized ShotDeck account when available. Download the originals for direct local preview; preserve each frame’s proportions and composition.
- **Literature:** Offer three verified covers or editions of the correct book. Identify edition, language or designer differences where established. Use flat front-cover artwork filling a fixed 600:927 portrait rectangle, with sharp corners, centered on a gray stage. Animal Farm C is the reference. Avoid photographed spines, perspective mockups, external margins, mock editions, AI-generated cover text and misidentified adaptations.
- **Photography:** Use one authentic original when only one faithful source image is available. Offer additional reproductions only when genuine source differences are useful. Preserve the full composition and original treatment. No artificial variations, crops, recoloring or AI recreations. A different exposure from the same event is another photograph, not an alternate reproduction. A lone image still needs an explicit choice. Follow [PHOTOGRAPHY_GUIDE.md](PHOTOGRAPHY_GUIDE.md).

Prefer original museum, publisher, label or archive files. Inspect every image. Record the source page, credit, actual pixel dimensions, edition/version where applicable and rights status. Show a concise description of the meaningful difference; keep detailed provenance expandable.

## Separate decisions

Image preference, writing approval and permission to publish are separate. An image choice never approves the prose. Approval of a draft does not silently transfer to a replacement image or edited text.

Keep chosen option IDs tied to the actual files and record revision. Changed chosen images require a new choice. A previously chosen image may remain selected when only alternative candidates change, provided its file, work identity, edition and visible description remain verified unchanged. Changed writing needs a new decision unless Julio expressly approves it subject to an exact requested edit. Record that condition and verify the edit; do not extend it to a broader rewrite. Never infer approval from a default or from the presence of an image in a preview.

## Feedback on passages

Selecting text in a story reveals Comment on selection. The editor keeps the selected quotation visible while Julio writes. Saved highlights reopen the comment; hovering previews it. Comments can be edited, resolved or reopened. General entry notes remain separate.

Each comment stores its original quotation, rendered-text offsets, surrounding context and body fingerprint. Selections may cross paragraphs within one entry. Selections spanning different entries or controls are not accepted. After a body revision, retain the comment with an Earlier draft label; do not silently attach it to a similar sentence.

Preserve saved decisions and notes when adding comment fields. Merge edits into the latest browser state and retain unsaved changes if storage fails. A later explicit approval may resolve earlier comments; record that evidence and retain each original quote and comment. Export includes open, resolved and earlier-draft comments. Test sessions must never write real approvals or export data marked ready for release.
