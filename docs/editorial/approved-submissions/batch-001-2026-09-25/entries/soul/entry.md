# Soul

Pete Docter; co-directed by Kemp Powers · 2020

![Joe Gardner appears in close-up with his eyes closed, surrounded by glowing blue and purple light.](assets/selected.png)

In *Soul* (2020), New York music teacher Joe Gardner believes his real life will begin when he becomes a professional jazz pianist. He loves playing, but years of unsuccessful auditions have made everything else feel secondary. His fear comes out in a conversation with his mother:

> I’m just afraid that if I died today, that my life would have amounted to nothing.

An accident separates Joe’s soul from his body on the day he finally lands a coveted gig. His body is still alive, but his soul is heading towards the Great Beyond, the film’s afterlife. Refusing to go, he escapes into the Great Before, where souls prepare for life on Earth. There he meets 22, who has never found a reason to want to be born. Repeated failures to discover a calling have left her feeling inadequate. Joe becomes her mentor and assumes that finding a passion will solve her problem.

When they find a way to Earth, a mix-up puts 22 in Joe’s body. She enjoys experiences he takes for granted: tasting pizza, watching a seed drift from a tree, talking with people. These encounters make her want more time on Earth before she has chosen anything to do with her life.

Joe struggles to value these experiences because he still measures a life’s worth by professional success. His love of music can be part of a worthwhile life without being its only justification.
