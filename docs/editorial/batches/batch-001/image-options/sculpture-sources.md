## Result and limits

The manifest contains three genuine source photographs for each object. No option is a color variant or resized duplicate of another. Native files retain their source pixels. `thumbnailWindow` specifies a nondestructive presentation crop; original files remain available.

David A is the only selectable generated treatment. It replaces the background and reframes a real photograph. Its broad identity remains, but small marble marks and hair texture vary. The original is linked locally for comparison. Do not describe it as an unchanged photograph.

The other eight selections are original photography. All three Nara sources look upward; no verified eye-level source was obtained. Their temple backgrounds remain. These are disclosed limits, not a claim that every image satisfies the requested neutral-background portrait direction. Bernini C also looks upward. Rai's paired-head source is soft. The native David C head crop has limited detail because its source is a wider view.

The generated Nara treatments were excluded from the selectable options. They retain recognizable identity but alter patina distribution and fine curl incisions. Preserving the object takes priority over making backgrounds uniform. The source photographs offer a more faithful review.
