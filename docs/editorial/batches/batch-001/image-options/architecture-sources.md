The three Pantheon scans come from the University of Tokyo's copy of the Firmin Didot Frères collected *Opere*, volume 6, 1835–1839. Commons dates the underlying etchings to 1790. These are separate plates, not alternate crops or tonal grades of one image.

[Brasil Arquitetura's project record](https://brasilarquitetura.com/project/sesc-fabrica-pompeia-lina) independently confirms SESC Pompeia and the team of Lina Bo Bardi, André Vainer, and Marcelo Ferraz. ArchEyes credits its reproduced drawings to Bo Bardi. That credit does not establish the individual hand or date of the elevation and plan.

## Access and fidelity

- Commons metadata and the downloaded Piranesi sheets were checked together. The drawing dates and later scanned edition are kept separate.
- The British Museum collection page returned 403 directly. Its indexed catalog text and official large image were accessible. The museum's qualified attribution and restoration label are preserved.
- The 1844 gateway plate comes from a community book archive. Its caption confirms the monument and view. It is the smallest Borobudur source and remains at its authentic resolution.
- The official Power Station of Art page requires JavaScript. Its published image was inspected directly; the same study also appears in the ArchEyes gallery. The source inscriptions support the work identity, section type, and 1977 date.
- SESC's elevation and site plan are clear but only 1000 and 1100 pixels wide. They have not been artificially enlarged. They also retain the project's plain modern drawing language instead of adding false historical ornament.
- Borobudur's faint 1873 partial lithographs were considered and rejected. The stronger selected drawings provide clearer, distinct views. Unused downloads remain preserved.

## Display and rights

`thumbnailWindow` is a normalized object `{x, y, w, h}`. It provides a reversible display window, not a cropped asset. These windows remove blank paper, a lower cartouche, or capture surroundings. They retain each selected architectural view. Full source files remain available through `originalImageUrl`.

All options carry `review_only_pending_clearance`. Pantheon sources carry public-domain designations on Commons. The Indonesian government diagram has a public-domain designation specific to its source. British Museum, book-archive, ArchEyes, and museum-published Bo Bardi reproductions have distinct rights conditions. Local review does not grant app-publication permission. Full credits, source URLs, download URLs, image dimensions, and SHA-256 values are recorded per option in the manifest.

No AI-generated images were required. No UI, release status, source-entry prose, or other image manifests were changed.
