## Antigone

- **A — National Theatre, 2012.** [Institutional image record](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/production-image-for-antigone-2012/XgFqOEdf-o3isw) identifies Jodie Whittaker as Antigone. [Production catalogue](https://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/calmview/Record.aspx?id=PERF8661&src=CalmView.Performance) confirms the Olivier production and Don Taylor version. The individual image record does not name the photographer. 800 × 1200.
- **B — National Theatre poster, 1984.** [Official archive shop](https://shop.nationaltheatre.org.uk/products/pod1033591) dates the poster. The inspected artwork names Sophocles, translator C. A. Trypanis, directors John Burgess and Peter Gill, and the Cottesloe Theatre. Official reproduction with its supplied white border; 1000 × 1000.
- **C — Harry translation, 1911.** [Commons scan record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Antigone_of_Sophocles_(1911).djvu); inspected title page verifies translator, publisher, city, and year. Public-domain scan; original copy Indiana University, Google digitization. 1280 × 2023.

## A Doll’s House

- **A — First-edition binding, 1879.** [SNL museum photograph](https://snl.no/Et_dukkehjem), by Jon-Erik Faksvaag / Ibsenmuseet, Norsk folkemuseum. CC BY-SA 3.0. 1084 × 1200.
- **B — Réjane as Nora, 1894.** [Oslo Museum record TM.T04662](https://digitaltmuseum.no/021016950843/gabrielle-rejane-i-et-dukkehjem) explicitly dates the photograph 1894 and credits Charles Reutlinger. CC BY-SA 4.0. Source record retrieved through public HTML when the web extractor failed. Image downloaded from SNL’s credited copy; 730 × 981.
- **C — Royal Theatre playbill, January 17, 1880.** [SNL](https://snl.no/Et_dukkehjem), credited to Antikvariat Bryggen, restricted reuse. The visible date governs the label. Do not call it the opening-night playbill. The initial download filename contains “premiere,” but manifest text records the actual date. 869 × 1200.

## Auto da Compadecida

- **A — Original Recife company.** [Teatrojornal archive photograph](https://teatrojornal.com.br/2015/06/60-anos-da-peleja-de-joao-grilo-e-chico/), credited to Projeto Memórias da Cena Pernambucana. Caption identifies Clênio Wanderley’s 1956 production; filename says 1957. Exact photograph date remains uncertain. Suassuna appears with the company. 960 × 450.
- **B — Maria Cutia, 2019.** [UFMG notice and photograph](https://www3.ufmg.br/comunicacao/noticias/grupo-maria-cutia-apresenta-espetaculo-o-auto-da-compadecida) credits Tati Motta. [Company production record](https://mariacutia.com.br/espetaculos/auto-da-compadecida/) identifies the director and photographer; [repertory](https://mariacutia.com.br/espetaculos/) lists the 2019 premiere. Exact photographic performance date unspecified. 3180 × 1318.
- **C — Nova Fronteira anniversary hardcover.** [Publisher record](https://www.novafronteira.com.br/auto-da-compadecida-comemorativa), ISBN 9786556400754. Catalog lists 44th edition (2024), marketed as the 70-year commemorative edition. This is a later-edition product image, not the first edition. 600 × 600 PNG with transparent background.
