**Two preservation invariants**

1. **Original source images remain available for comparison** – every new study keeps a link to its original image (`originalImageUrl`) so that the editorial record can be verified against the source.  
2. **Fingerprints of the 12‑entry editorial batch stay unchanged** – the round 3 visual studies do not alter the hashes of the underlying editorial entries; the batch’s integrity is preserved.  

These invariants are referenced in the README’s “Verification” section and are enforced by the `build_section()` logic that checks file existence and computes fingerprints.
