## Source access and critical challenges

The JSON gives precise URLs and claim mappings for every entry. Institutional/critical pages were opened and read unless the individual mapping explicitly says indexed text. Accademia’s direct page timed out, although its institutional catalogue text was returned by search. Opera del Duomo’s indexed account corroborates the commission and republican placement. Baldwin’s five-page David essay was read, but its later Medici/papal chronology has material errors; those passages and their downstream political claims are excluded.

The Borghese catalogue essay supplies both object documentation and substantive interpretation. The whole original saved myth provides additional user-supplied narrative context. The temple’s two detailed pages supply history and an explicit religious interpretation, rather than an outside claim that the object is merely imperial propaganda. A public discussion of Tōdai-ji confused building dates and asserted economic ruin; those claims were rejected.

Beethoven-Haus’s German work record and Eastman’s substantial musical analysis were read. ABC’s indexed listening guide corroborated accessible formal description. The draft interprets the movement from instrumental to vocal participation; it does not say Beethoven documented that exact explanatory wording as his intention.

The Library of Congress Julia Blackburn essay was read earlier in this research pass. Later reopens returned 403. Its uncertain lynching-photograph identification, pseudonym/name-change wording, and speculative persecution connection are not repeated. Smithsonian’s Geoffrey Himes essay and Indiana Public Media’s David Johnson article were read. The latter independently verifies Café Society’s special performance rules. No song lyrics are quoted.

IMS catalogue and Luiz Fernando Vianna’s article were read. The Redalyc route to Gabriel S. S. Lima Rezende’s paper returned 403; the SciELO full text was accessible, and its relevant formal-analysis sections were read. The larger national-modernization thesis remains an author’s interpretation and is not imported wholesale. Ricardo Quiñones’s performance analysis supplies the description of guitar and voice. Its biographical anecdotes and simplistic sole-inventor assertions are excluded. Senado’s commemorative article was also consulted, but its incorrect Orfeu chronology was excluded and it is not necessary to the final draft.

Public reception checks were bounded samples: David discussion concentrated on greatness/technique; Apollo visitors on movement and viewing angles; Tōdai-ji on scale and consecration; Beethoven listeners on hearing the entire work; Holiday listeners on identifying the lynching metaphor and distinguishing versions; Gilberto listeners on longing mixed with hope. Some contained clear misinformation. None of those posts establishes an approval, consensus or factual claim in the bodies.
